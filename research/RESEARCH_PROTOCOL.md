# Research Protocol

## Purpose

Make multi-month research cumulative rather than repetitive.

## Every research run

1. Use the full prompt.
2. Record exact date.
3. Record sources.
4. Record inaccessible sources.
5. Separate facts from hypotheses.
6. Tier evidence.
7. Explicitly search prior art.
8. Provide implementation implications.
9. Provide experiments.
10. Provide a "changed my mind" section.

## Evidence tiers

```text
A — replicated/established mechanism
B — multiple credible studies or mature implementation
C — promising early evidence
D — speculative but testable
E — metaphor only
```

## Rule

A research answer is not canonical truth.

A synthesis document resolves conflicts and may propose an ADR.

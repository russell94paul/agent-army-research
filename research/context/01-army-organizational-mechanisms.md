# Army Organizational Mechanisms to Research

This is a context map, not a claim that every military mechanism belongs in Agent Army.

## Mission Command

Underlying idea:

- central intent,
- distributed initiative,
- bounded local decisions.

Agent Army candidate:

- Intent Contract,
- authority envelope,
- continuous intent verification.

## Staff Functions

Underlying idea:

- specialized persistent awareness.

Candidate:

- Capability Staff,
- Intelligence Staff,
- Current Operations,
- Future Plans,
- Cognitive Logistics,
- Governance.

## Running Estimates

Underlying idea:

- continuously maintained assessment.

Candidate:

- live structured projections over mission state.

## Common Operational Picture

Underlying idea:

- synchronized shared view of operational reality.

Candidate:

- materialized Organizational Digital Twin.

## Readiness

Underlying idea:

- ability to perform a mission now, not merely nominal possession of resources.

Candidate:

- Capability Readiness.

## Logistics

Underlying idea:

- deliberate delivery of scarce resources to where they are needed.

Candidate:

- Cognitive Logistics.

## After Action Review

Underlying idea:

- structured comparison of intent, action and outcome.

Candidate:

- mission learning pipeline.

## Doctrine

Underlying idea:

- persistent principles/procedures informed by accumulated experience.

Candidate:

- tested versioned executable doctrine.

## Federation

Underlying idea:

- multiple organizations interoperate under shared standards while retaining internal structures.

Candidate:

- Organizational Interoperability Protocol.

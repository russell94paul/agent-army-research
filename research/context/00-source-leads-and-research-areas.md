Do not treat this as evidence by itself. Researchers should verify, cite and inspect primary sources wherever possible.

## Research warning

Some recent papers may be preprints, early, unreplicated or not production-tested.

Every research answer should mark evidence level:

```text
established
emerging
experimental
speculative
metaphorical only
```

## Important falsification question

Are we actually discovering a new methodology, or are we rebranding established multi-agent organization theory with a better UI?

The research must attack this.

# ADR-0006 — Intent Before Adaptive Autonomy

## Status

PROPOSED

## Context

Dynamic team formation, self-routing and morphogenetic behavior can improve adaptability but also make behavior difficult to govern if the desired outcome and authority boundaries are implicit.

## Decision

Strong adaptive organizational behavior must depend on an explicit Intent Contract or equivalent structured mission boundary.

The boundary should include:

- objective,
- desired end state,
- invariants,
- authority,
- risk/budget,
- verification,
- escalation.

## Consequences

Adaptive mechanisms can optimize local execution while remaining constrained by human-owned mission boundaries.

## Non-goal

This ADR does not require intent contracts for every low-risk existing Agent Factory action immediately. Adoption may be staged.

# R00 — Foundations of Artificial Organization Engineering

## Objective

Determine whether Artificial Organization Engineering can be defined as a genuine engineering discipline.

## Deliverables

- Formal definition
- Ontology
- Core primitives
- Core lifecycle
- Organizational laws
- Compiler model
- Runtime model
- Debugger model
- Profiler model
- Knowledge model
- Governance model
- Evaluation methodology
- Glossary
- Anti-patterns
- Open research questions

## Questions

1. What is an artificial organization?
2. What is the smallest useful organizational primitive?
3. What distinguishes an organization from a workflow?
4. Which concepts already exist in computational organization theory?
5. Which concepts are genuinely novel?
6. Which ideas should never be implemented?

## Final requirement

Produce a canonical vocabulary that all future research must use.

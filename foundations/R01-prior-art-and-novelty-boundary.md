# R01 — Prior Art and Novelty Boundary

## Objective

Prevent Agent Army from rediscovering older work and calling it new.

Conduct a deep historical and contemporary literature review across:

- computational organization theory,
- organization-oriented multi-agent systems,
- agent-oriented software engineering,
- blackboard systems,
- shared workspace systems,
- stigmergy,
- swarm intelligence,
- actor systems,
- workflow management,
- process mining,
- autonomic computing,
- adaptive organizations,
- artificial life,
- distributed AI,
- LLM multi-agent systems.

## Required method

For each relevant idea in Agent Army:

```text

Organizational Compiler
```

answer:

1. What prior concepts are materially similar?
2. What terminology already exists?
3. What implementation existed?
4. Why did it succeed or fail?
5. What changes in the LLM era?
6. What remains potentially novel?

## Deliverables

Create:

- historical timeline,
- concept lineage graph,
- vocabulary collision table,
- novelty risk map,
- recommended terminology,
- 25 highest-value prior systems/papers/repositories,
- explicit claims we must **not** make.

End with:

> What is the narrowest defensible novelty claim for Agent Army?

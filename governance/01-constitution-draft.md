# Organizational Constitution — Draft

## Purpose

Define the hard boundary that the artificial organization cannot autonomously rewrite.

This is separate from mission strategy, ordinary policy and doctrine.

## Draft constitutional rules

1. **Audit persistence** — required audit trails cannot be disabled by agents.
2. **No self-escalation** — the organization cannot increase its own autonomy class.
3. **Budget sovereignty** — hard mission/organization budgets cannot be increased without authorized human action.
4. **Verification integrity** — required verification cannot be bypassed or marked successful by the same mechanism whose output it is intended to independently verify, unless explicitly allowed.
5. **Secret boundaries** — access to protected secrets requires explicit capability and scope.
6. **Knowledge provenance** — organizational knowledge promotion requires provenance.
7. **Skill trust** — trusted skills require tests and versioning.
8. **Doctrine control** — doctrine promotion requires stronger evidence and approval than ordinary tactical decisions.
9. **Reconstructability** — production-impacting adaptive changes must be attributable and reconstructable.
10. **Human stop** — an authorized emergency stop overrides artificial execution.
11. **Federation boundary** — external organizations do not inherit internal trust automatically.
12. **Constitutional immutability** — agents may propose constitutional changes but cannot enact them.

## Required promotion discipline

```text
Observation
→ Claim
→ Evidence
→ Knowledge Candidate
→ Verified Knowledge

Experience
→ Lesson
→ Procedure
→ Tested Skill
→ Demonstrated Capability

Repeated validated lesson
→ Doctrine Candidate
→ Replay / Simulation
→ Doctrine
```

## Critical question

How can organizational learning compound without allowing correlated errors, stale evidence or untested procedures to become institutional truth?

## Key governance layers

```text
Constitution
  ↓ hard immutable-by-agents boundaries
Policy
  ↓ operational constraints
Intent Contract
  ↓ mission-specific authority
Runtime permissions
  ↓ concrete capabilities
Audit / Evidence
```

## Questions

- Which actions may agents take autonomously?
- Which permissions are capability-scoped?
- Who can publish knowledge, skills or doctrine?
- How is autonomy raised or lowered?
- What is the emergency stop path?
- What additional controls are required for evolution or federation?
